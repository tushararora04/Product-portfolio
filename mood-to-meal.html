From “I don’t
know” to
that’s it.

A mood-led food discovery flow that turns a vague craving into a small, confident set of dinner options.

Choice is useful. Too much choice is work.

Food delivery apps offer an endless aisle of possibilities. When a user is hungry and unsure, filtering can feel like another task. This concept trades browsing for a brief guided conversation: voice or quiz input, then three to five highly relevant choices.

~5Minutes target to order
15%Month-one adoption target
10%Cart-abandonment reduction target
The MVP

Rules before models.

The smallest credible test is a tightly defined, rule-based flow that maps mood, occasion and dietary preferences to a limited set of restaurant and dish options.

PRODUCT DECISION

Show 3-5 choices, not another infinite feed.

When no match exists, relax filters one at a time. A graceful fallback feels more helpful than a dead end.

What success means

Less browsing. More “order placed.”

Judge this by a faster menu-open-to-order journey, feature adoption, and reduced abandonment among users who indicate uncertainty.
